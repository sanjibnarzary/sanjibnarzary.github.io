## Full Description

**ScanFlow: Docs & Book Scanner** (Narzary Scan) is a high-precision, privacy-first document scanner and PDF studio engineered by **Dr. Sanjib Narzary** (PhD, IIT Guwahati). It combines high-speed multi-page scanning, an in-app PDF and image reader, smooth edge cropping, a precision optical loupe, and optional Google Drive synchronization into a dedicated "Narzary Doc Scan" folder.

### Key Highlights:
✓ **In-App PDF & Image Viewer:** Read and inspect saved documents with smooth 100%–450% pinch-to-zoom and multi-page thumbnail navigation.  
✓ **Direct "Edit Edges" Tool:** Easily adjust crop boundaries on already saved PDFs and images with native `PdfRenderer` high-resolution rendering.  
✓ **Multi-Page Continuous Scanning:** Snap multiple document pages in the camera continuously before entering the editor.  
✓ **Precision Optical Loupe Lens:** 2.5× optical magnifying loupe appears only when moving edges or vertices for sub-pixel alignment.  
✓ **Ultra-Smooth 120Hz Edge Dragging:** Drag any of the 4 edges or 4 corners with zero lag or toast feedback interruptions.  
✓ **Multi-Lingual Digitize (OCR):** Supports all 22 scheduled Indian languages (Assamese, Bodo, Bengali, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and more) plus international scripts.  
✓ **Optional Google Cloud Drive Sync:** Connect Google Drive to automatically back up all scanned PDFs and images in your dedicated "Narzary Doc Scan" folder.  
✓ **12-Tool Studio Suite:** Filters (*Auto Color*, *B&W High Contrast*, *Whiteboard*), Magic Eraser, Brightness/Contrast, Markup Highlighter, and Page Organizer.  
✓ **100% Local & Encrypted:** Built-in optional AES-256-GCM vault password protection. Zero third-party telemetry.  
✓ **Comprehensive Settings Hub:** Dark/Light theme, Liquid Display 120Hz mode, Material You dynamic colors, Google Drive sync, and Dr. Sanjib Narzary developer biography.  
✓ **Google Play In-App Billing (8.1.0+):** Upgrade to Pro with transparent 1-month, 1-year, or lifetime support plans to permanently remove watermarks.
