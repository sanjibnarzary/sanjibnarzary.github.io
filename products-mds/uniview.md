# Narzary Doc Viewer (OmniDoc) 📱📑

> **Universal Document, Office, Spreadsheet, Presentation, Code, Markdown & Image Suite for Android**  
> **Architected by Dr. Sanjib Narzary**

Narzary Doc Viewer (OmniDoc) is a high-performance, local-first Android productivity suite built with Jetpack Compose and Material Design 3. It provides dedicated multi-window intent activities tailored for every document format, enabling seamless viewing and editing directly from system "Open With" intents or the in-app document manager.

---

## 🌟 Major Highlights & Modules

### 📝 Word & Document Viewer & In-Built Editor (`.docx`, `.doc`, `.rtf`)
- **Page-by-Page Realistic Reading:** Displays documents page by page with running headers, page counters, and clean paper shadows.
- **Google Docs Style Live Formatter:**
  - Block styles: Title, Section (H1), Subsection (H2), and Normal body.
  - Character styles: **Bold**, *Italic*, <u>Underline</u>, and ~~Strikethrough~~.
  - Text alignment: Left, Center, Right, and Justify.
  - Lists: Bullet points (`•`) and auto-numbered items (`1.`).
  - Google Docs color palette for colored accents.
  - Font size controls (`10sp` to `32sp`).
- **Rich Document Elements:** Renders formatted headers, tables (3×3 grids), analytical metric bar charts, system architecture flowchart diagrams, and embedded images.
- **Direct Live Editing:** Tap directly into any paragraph, heading, or table cell on any page to edit text in place without external popups.

### 📊 Excel & Spreadsheets (`.xlsx`, `.xls`, `.csv`, `.tsv`)
- **Unblocked 2D Horizontal & Vertical Scrolling:** Smooth navigation through extensive rows and columns without gesture blocking.
- **Sticky Column & Row Headers:** Sticky row numbers (#) on the left and sticky column letters (A, B, C...) at the top.
- **Google Sheets Style Formula Bar:** Real-time formula bar showing raw cell values and formulas. Quick formula chips for `=SUM()`, `=AVERAGE()`, `=COUNT()`, `=MAX()`, `=MIN()`, and `=IF()`.
- **Direct Cell Matrix:** Tap any cell to select; double-tap for cell editing dialog with clear and save controls.
- **Multi-Sheet Workbook Tabs:** Add, switch, and view multiple sheets in XLSX workbooks.

### 📽️ Presentations & Slides (`.pptx`, `.ppt`, `.odp`)
- **Dual Presentation Experience:**
  - **Continuous Feed:** Vertical slide feed with slide number badges and speaker notes.
  - **Present Mode (Theater Slideshow):** Tap the **Present** icon for a cinematic black-stage slideshow displaying slides **one by one** with horizontal transition animations, previous/next controls, gesture swipes, and an auto-play timer (5s per slide).

### 🖼️ Photos & Image Viewer with Google Photos Style Editor
- **Google Photos Style Editing Suite:**
  - **Crop:** Freeform crop and presets (Square 1:1, 16:9, 4:3, 3:2).
  - **Rotate & Flip:** 90° clockwise rotation, fine rotation angle slider (-45° to +45°), horizontal and vertical flip.
  - **Resize:** Scale presets (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) and custom pixel dimension scaling.
  - **Adjustments:** Real-time Brightness, Contrast, and Saturation adjustments.
  - **Multi-Format Export:** Save edited images as **PNG**, **JPG**, or **WEBP** with adjustable compression quality.
- **Star & Favorites:** Star/favorite photos with instant toggle and filtering.

### 🔒 Password-Protected PDFs (`.pdf`)
- **Encrypted PDF Prompt:** Automatically detects password-protected PDFs and displays a secure password input dialog with password visibility toggle.
- **Continuous Multi-Page Rendering:** High-resolution vertical page feed with smooth pinch-to-zoom and pan gestures.

### 💻 Programming Code & Text Viewer
- **Multi-Language Support:** C, C++, Java, Kotlin, Python, JSON, XML, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, Bash, and Plain Text.
- **Syntax Highlighting & Line Numbers:** Semantic syntax highlighting with synchronized line number gutter.
- **Pinch-to-Zoom Font Scale:** Two-finger pinch gesture adjusts code font size dynamically.
- **JSON Pretty-Printer:** Formats minified JSON with 2-space indentation at a single tap.

### 🪟 Multi-Window Intent Architecture & Clean Back Navigation
- Every file type (Word, Excel, PPT, PDF, Code, Images, Markdown) opens in its own dedicated Android intent window in the system task carousel.
- **Clean Back Navigation:** Pressing the top bar back arrow or the Android hardware/gesture back button immediately closes the document window and returns cleanly to the calling app.

## 👨‍💻 About Dr. Sanjib Narzary

### About Dr. Sanjib Narzary
Dr. Sanjib Narzary holds a Doctor of Philosophy degree from the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati. He earned a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering from the National Institute of Technology Silchar and a Master of Technology in Computer Science and Information Security from the National Institute of Technology Calicut.

### Technology and projects
Passionate about technology, Sanjib enjoys building small, practical projects in his spare time. He created Echo Search, a personal AI search engine, and Alayaran, a personalized news reader powered by RSS feeds.

His mobile apps include PetPal, a virtual assistant for pet lovers; GPSCameraMap, for GPS-tagged images and videos; FluidClean, for clearing junk and temporary files; AnyDocViewer, for viewing documents, source code, and PDFs; and Kidz Learn, a learning app for children in India with support for 22 languages. Other projects include QuoteCraft for sharing quotes as images or videos, PDF Studio for managing PDF pages and files, Video Converter for format conversion and compression, and a QR and barcode scanner and generator. Most of his creation can be found here Products.

### Community and service
Originally from a remote village in Assam’s Kokrajhar district, Sanjib believes that where you come from should never limit what you dream of or create. He is proud of his community and the Bodo language. He has contributed to and translated open-source software for LibreOffice, Fedora, GNOME, and Mozilla. His open-source projects are available on GitHub.

He serves on the Board of Governors at CIT Kokrajhar and has served as Head of its Department of Computer Science and Engineering. He is a member of the Bodo Sahitya Sabha, the Linguistic Society of India, the Robotics Society of India, the Association for Computing Machinery, and IEEE. He is also Co-Principal Investigator of the MeitY, Government of India-funded “Multilevel Capacity Building and Skilling in Industry-Aligned Emerging Technologies” project, and has supported Subunthi Sibinai, a free computer-training initiative under the Unnat Bharat Abhiyan mission.

### Personal & Family Life
Outside his work, he enjoys family life with his wife and two daughters.
