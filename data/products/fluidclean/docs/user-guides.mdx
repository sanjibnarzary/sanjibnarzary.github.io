# FluidClean User Guide & Manual 📱

Welcome to FluidClean, your intelligent Android storage optimizer and battery health companion. This guide provides step-by-step instructions to get the most out of your app.

---

## 1. Quick Start: One-Tap Cleanup
1. Open FluidClean to the **Dashboard**.
2. Review your current **Storage Usage** gauge and **System Health Score**.
3. Tap the vibrant **One-Tap Clean** button.
4. Watch the animated rocket booster launch from the middle bottom, flare up its booster, and accelerate to the top of the screen.
5. The overlay card displays your reclaimed megabytes and the percentage improvement. Your available storage gauge updates immediately.

---

## 2. Deep Duplicate Photos & Documents Cleaning
1. From the Dashboard or bottom navigation, select **Clean** (or tap the Duplicate banner).
2. Browse the categorized tabs:
   - **Photos**: Identifies burst copies, social media export duplicates, and camera image duplicates.
   - **Docs**: Identifies duplicate PDFs, Word documents, APK remnants, and compressed archives.
   - **Cache**: Displays application temporary caches and system buffers.
3. Review the green **ORIGINAL (KEEP)** badge protecting your primary copy, and red checkboxes marking duplicate copies.
4. Select or deselect individual copies, or tap **Select All Duplicates**.
5. Tap **Clean Selected Clutter** to permanently delete the redundant files from disk and reclaim storage.

---

## 3. Battery Health Telemetry Dashboard
1. On the **Dashboard**, view the liquid wave battery capsule displaying live charge percentage, thermal temperature in °C and °F, and estimated remaining usage time.
2. Tap the card to open **Battery Analytics**:
   - Live charging speed (Rapid AC, USB, Wireless, or Discharging).
   - Core hardware temperature monitor (with thermal safety indicators).
   - Battery voltage telemetry (in mV).
   - Toggle **Smart Ultra Power Saver** to throttle background sync and extend battery endurance.

---

## 4. Floating Quick Clean Bubble
1. Open **Settings** -> **Floating Quick Clean Icon**.
2. Toggle the switch ON. If prompted, allow "Display over other apps" in Android System Settings.
3. A glowing circular bubble with a rocket icon will appear on your screen.
4. You can drag the bubble to any edge of your screen.
5. Tap the bubble anytime while browsing or gaming to execute a quick boost with rocket launch animation.

---

## 5. Language & Regional Settings
1. Go to **Settings** -> **App Language**.
2. Select from **all 22 official scheduled languages of India** (with Bodo positioned right after Hindi) or major international languages.
3. The application interface updates instantly.
