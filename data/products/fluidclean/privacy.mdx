# FluidClean Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: October 2026**

Your privacy is paramount. FluidClean is built with a fundamental commitment to zero personal data harvesting.

---

### 1. Zero Cloud Data Collection
- **On-Device Only**: FluidClean performs all file scanning, binary comparisons, duplicate grouping, cache clearance, and battery health telemetry strictly on your local Android device.
- **No Remote Servers**: No photographs, video files, personal documents, contact data, location metrics, or identifying hardware IDs are ever uploaded, transmitted, or backed up to remote cloud servers.

### 2. Device Permissions Usage
- **Storage / Files Access**: Required solely to identify duplicate photos, documents, and temporary junk files in accessible directories on your device.
- **System Alert Window (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`)**: Required only when you enable the optional Floating Quick Clean icon to display the booster bubble over your screen.
- **Vibration (`VIBRATE`)**: Used solely to provide tactile haptic pulses when cleanup operations and boosts complete.
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)**: Used solely to notify you when cleaning operations conclude or when storage reaches critical capacity.
- **Accessibility Service (Optional Deep Boost)**: If enabled by the user, Android Accessibility API is utilized solely on-device to automate temporary application cache clearance. FluidClean does not inspect, read, or log keystrokes, personal messages, or credentials.

### 3. Google Play Billing
When subscribing to or purchasing FluidClean Pro, transactions are processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing (Google LLC). FluidClean does not store or access your credit card numbers, bank credentials, or billing details.

### 4. Local Database Storage
All optimization logs, timestamped cleaning sessions, and user configuration preferences are stored in a private local SQLite database on your device using Android Room. Clearing the app data or uninstalling the app deletes all local records completely.
