# Privacy Policy

ScreenCaster is described as a tool for casting an Android device screen to a television. Screen casting can expose whatever is visible on the device display to the connected screen; close or hide sensitive content before starting a cast.

The available product information does not specify what device permissions, connection services, analytics, diagnostics, or retention practices are used. Review the Android screen-capture prompt, the app's permission requests, and the current in-app privacy disclosures before casting.

If you make an in-app purchase, the payment provider displayed at checkout processes the transaction under its own privacy policy.
