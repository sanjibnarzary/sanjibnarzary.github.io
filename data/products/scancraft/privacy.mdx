# Privacy Policy

ScanCraft uses the camera to read codes and can store scan results for later reference.

## Scan results and local history

The product information describes scan results and product profiles being cached in a local Room database. A result may contain information encoded in a QR code or barcode, such as a URL, identifier, or product details. Treat saved results as potentially sensitive and delete them when no longer needed.

## Product lookups

Product lookup can contact services such as Open Food Facts or UPC Lookup to retrieve product information. Information included in a lookup request is shared with the service that handles it and is subject to that service's policies.

## Permissions and purchases

Camera access is needed to scan codes. Google Play processes in-app purchases where offered. The product brief does not specify other analytics or diagnostic practices; review the app's disclosures for the installed version.
