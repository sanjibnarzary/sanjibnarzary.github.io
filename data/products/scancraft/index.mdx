## Scan and look up

ScanCraft reads QR codes and supported retail and logistics barcode formats. Web links in QR results can be opened from the result screen. Product lookups can retrieve product details from online services, while saved scans are cached on the device for later reference.

## Save and share results

Copy, share, or export a scan as text or an image card. Add notes or a stock quantity to a result when keeping an inventory record.

## Create custom codes

Generate QR codes with a centered logo and create supported barcode formats, including EAN, UPC, Code 128, Data Matrix, PDF417, and Aztec. Save or share the generated codes.
