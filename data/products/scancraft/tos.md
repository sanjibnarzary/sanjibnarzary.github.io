- **Audio Capture & Export:** When video recording is enabled by the user in settings, the application records synchronized AAC audio along with video at target frame rates. All saved and exported videos retain the synchronized audio track.
- **Camera View Audio Controls:** An interactive sound icon in the camera view enables users to toggle audio recording on or off at any time, including during active recording.
- **Normal Speed Guarantee:** Video and audio are captured and played back in real-time normal speed (1.0x). If configured by the user in settings, alternative playback speeds (0.5x, 1.5x, 2.0x) may be selected.
