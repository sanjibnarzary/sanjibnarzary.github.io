# About ScanCraft

ScanCraft combines QR and barcode scanning with product lookup, scan history, and custom code generation. It is designed for personal code scanning as well as keeping lightweight product and inventory records.

## Scan, save, and generate

Supported features include QR and retail barcode scanning, optional online product lookups, locally cached scan results, notes and quantity fields, and QR or barcode generation. Results can be copied, shared, or exported.
