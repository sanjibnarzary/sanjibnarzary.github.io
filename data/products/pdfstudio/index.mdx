## Manage PDF pages and content

- Merge documents, split selected page ranges, and reorder, rotate, or remove pages.
- Add annotations, highlights, text, page numbers, or a custom watermark.
- Compress PDFs and extract text from scanned pages with on-device OCR.
- Sign documents and redact sensitive content.

## Scan and convert documents

Scan paper documents to PDF, convert office files and images, or export PDF pages as images or text. The built-in file library helps find, organize, rename, and reopen documents stored on the device.

## Offline-first workflow

The product brief describes its PDF tools and AI-assisted document features as on-device and offline. The free edition adds a Narzary PDF Studio watermark to saved documents; the Pro edition removes it.
