# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

This policy explains how FieldCam GPS: Map Stamp Camera ("the app") handles your information.

## On-device processing

Photos, videos, GPS coordinates, compass and altitude readings, and offline maps are processed and saved locally on your device. No personal location history is sent to any external server without your consent.

## Permissions we request

- **Camera:** to capture photos and videos.
- **Location:** to stamp coordinates, address and the MiniMap on your media. Location is only accessed while the app is in use or during an action you start. There is no background tracking.
- **Microphone:** to record audio with video. You can turn audio off at any time from the camera view.

## No ads or trackers

The app contains no third-party advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

## Payments

Pro subscriptions are processed by Google Play In-App Billing. We do not collect or store your card number or payment credentials. Voluntary donations are made through your own UPI app and handled by your bank and UPI provider.

## Your control

You can revoke camera, location or microphone permissions in your device settings at any time. Deleting your photos and videos, or uninstalling the app, removes the data it stored on your device.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest revision.
