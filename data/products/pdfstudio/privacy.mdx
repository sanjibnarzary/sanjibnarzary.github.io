# Privacy Policy

The submitted Narzary PDF Studio Pro information describes an offline, on-device workflow for PDF and document tools.

## Documents and processing

Files selected for reading, scanning, editing, OCR, conversion, signing, redaction, or summarization may contain sensitive information. The product brief describes these operations and AI-assisted features as offline. Documents are managed through on-device storage and a local Room database.

## Permissions and sharing

The app needs access to files you choose to open or create, and camera access when scanning paper documents. If you export or share a document, it is sent to the destination you select.

## Purchases and disclosures

Google Play processes in-app purchases where offered. Google Play handles payment credentials under its own policies. Consult the installed app's current disclosures for permission and data practices beyond those described in the submitted product information.
