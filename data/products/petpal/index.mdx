## A companion that stays with you

PetPal displays an animated pet over other apps. Move it around the screen, minimize it to a small bubble, and choose from multiple companions and accessories. The pet can react to music playback and perform animated actions such as walking, sleeping, eating, and dancing.

## Small steps toward focus

- Organize tasks into work, study, wellness, personal, and habit categories.
- Earn digital treats for completing tasks and use them to care for your pet.
- Use motivational quotes, voice commands, and text-to-speech interaction.
- Set eye-break, hydration, or posture reminders and review recent task progress.
