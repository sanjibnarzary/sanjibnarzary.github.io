# Privacy Policy

This page summarizes data practices described in the PetPal Assistant product information.

## Tasks, progress, and settings

Tasks and daily productivity statistics are stored in an on-device Room database. Pet selection, accessories, settings, and purchase flags are stored locally using Android DataStore.

## Overlay, audio, and voice

The floating pet uses Android's system overlay permission. Music detection responds to device audio playback. Voice commands use Android speech recognition, and spoken quotes use text-to-speech; these features may require microphone or audio-related permissions and depend on the Android services configured on the device.

## Purchases

PetPal describes in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes payments under its own privacy policy. The product brief does not specify whether speech recognition sends audio to an external service; review Android's speech-recognition disclosures and the app's current permission prompts.
