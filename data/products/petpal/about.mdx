# About PetPal Assistant

PetPal Assistant is an animated virtual pet that can float over other apps. It combines companion animations with a local task manager, digital treats, motivational quotes, voice interaction, and wellness reminders.

## Companion and productivity features

Choose a pet and accessories, move it around the screen, or minimize it to a floating bubble. Completing tasks can earn treats for the pet. The app also describes music-reactive animations, voice commands, eye-break reminders, and a productivity dashboard.
