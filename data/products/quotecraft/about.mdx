# About QuoteCraft

QuoteCraft is a creative studio for quote graphics, typography posters, and multi-screen video stories. Start with a photo, a camera capture, or a color canvas, then style the composition and export it to share.

## Create and share

The app includes canvas shapes and aspect ratios, text and avatar overlays, transitions, optional music, and a gallery for organizing finished images and videos. Its product information describes a watermark on free exports and supporter options for watermark removal and creator branding.
