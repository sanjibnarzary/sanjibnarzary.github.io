# Privacy Policy

QuoteCraft works with content you choose for creating quote graphics and video stories.

## Photos, camera, and exports

You can choose photos from the Android photo picker or capture a new image with the camera. Created images and rendered videos may contain your photos, text, avatar, creator branding, and other content you add. Review exports before sharing them.

## Optional audio and sharing

Ambient music is optional, and you can attach audio selected from your device. Sharing sends the selected export to the destination you choose.

## Purchases and data disclosures

The product brief describes Google Play Billing for supporter options. Google Play handles payment credentials under its policies. The available brief does not specify analytics, diagnostics, or retention practices; refer to the installed app's current privacy disclosures for those details.
