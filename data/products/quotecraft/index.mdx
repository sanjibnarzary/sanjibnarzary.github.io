## Create quote art

Choose a portrait, landscape, or square canvas and start with a photo, a camera capture, a solid color, or a gradient. Add quote text, style its typography, and use shapes or a photo avatar to make the composition your own.

## Build a multi-screen story

Arrange quote screens into a sequence, set each slide's duration, and choose transitions. Preview the story before exporting it as an MP4 video; optional ambient tracks or audio from your device can accompany it.

## Save and share creations

Browse, search, rename, favorite, or share finished images and videos from the built-in gallery. A QuoteCraft watermark appears on free exports; supporter options allow watermark removal and creator branding.
