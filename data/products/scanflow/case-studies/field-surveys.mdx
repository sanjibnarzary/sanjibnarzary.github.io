# Documenting field surveys

<HeroQuote>
	Every photo carries its location and time, so site reports practically write themselves.
</HeroQuote>

<br />

**How do you use GPS Camera Map?**

I photograph sites during surveys and rely on the GPS, address and date stamp on each image.

<br />

**How has it helped?**

No more guessing where a photo was taken, and the map preview makes reports easy to verify.

<br />

**What do you like most?**

It is quick to use in the field and the stamp is clear and easy to read.
