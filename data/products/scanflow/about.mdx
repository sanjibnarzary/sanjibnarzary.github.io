# About Narzary Scan

Narzary Scan, presented in its product information as ScanFlow: Docs & Book Scanner, helps digitize paper records and manage the resulting scans. Its tools cover continuous multi-page capture, edge correction, image cleanup, OCR, an in-app PDF and image viewer, and optional Google Drive backup.

## Designed for document workflows

- Scan documents, ID cards, and books, including multiple pages in one session.
- Refine page edges with a precision loupe and adjust scan appearance.
- Review and organize saved PDFs and images, and extract text with OCR.
- Optionally back up scans to a dedicated Google Drive folder.

The product information describes optional AES-256-GCM vault protection for selected files and local OCR processing.
