# Privacy Policy

This page describes the data handling disclosed in the available Narzary Scan (ScanFlow) product information.

## Scans and OCR

Document capture, edge adjustment, scan editing, and OCR are described as on-device features. Scans and recognized text may contain personal or sensitive information; review the files you choose to create, save, or share.

## Optional storage and backup

The app describes an optional AES-256-GCM vault for protecting selected files. Google Drive synchronization is optional and sends selected backups to the connected Google account and Google's service, subject to Google's terms and privacy policy.

## Permissions and purchases

Camera access is needed to capture pages. Other access is requested when you select files or use a particular device feature. If you make an in-app purchase, Google Play processes the transaction; payment credentials are handled by Google Play.

The available product information does not specify analytics, diagnostics, or retention outside the features described above. Consult the app's current disclosures and permission prompts for the installed version.
