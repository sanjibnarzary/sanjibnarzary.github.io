# FieldCam GPS - Release Notes

## Version 2.4.0 (Build 24) - October 2026

### 🚀 What's New & Highlights

#### 1. Reimplemented Subscription Model: $9.99 / Year (Google Play Merchant)
- **Annual Subscription:** Replaced legacy purchase methods with a streamlined annual auto-renewing subscription at **$9.99 / year** (~$0.83/month) via Google Play In-App Billing.
- **Auto-Renewal & Expiry Lifecycle:** Transparent billing policy conforming with Google Play Merchant policies.
- **Expiration Security:** In accordance with licensing rules, if an annual subscription reaches its expiration date without renewal, unlocked premium features and custom watermark editing automatically revert to locked status.
- **Manage & Restore Purchases:** One-tap navigation to the Google Play Store subscription management portal and instant purchase receipt restoration.

#### 2. Enhanced Donation Flow & Thank You Connection Modal
- **Personalized Thank You Modal:** Successful donations now trigger an engaging, heartfelt modal window highlighting the user's tangible contribution toward server costs, map bandwidth, and privacy-first ad-free development.
- **Watermark Unlock:** Immediately unlocks the locked Copyright watermark and custom watermark text in Settings for active donors!
- **UPI QR Code & Presets:** Native QR code scan card for UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) with UPI VPA `8822465418@sbi` and preset amounts (₹10, ₹50, ₹100, ₹250, ₹500).

#### 3. Watermark & Copyright Customization
- Customize watermark text, corner placement, and transparency once unlocked.
- Free tier locked indicator with quick-action upgrade and donation links.

#### 4. Landscape Layout Refinement
- MiniMap and comprehensive telemetry HUD seamlessly positioned on the left side.
- Survey note badge cleanly anchored to the right edge with quick-tap editing.

#### 5. Documentation & Policy Compliance
- Fully updated `TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md`, `LICENSE`, `README.md`, and `PLAY_STORE_LISTING.md`.
