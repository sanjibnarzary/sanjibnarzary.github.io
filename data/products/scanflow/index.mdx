## Capture and refine pages

- Scan multiple pages continuously before moving to the editor.
- Adjust page edges with a precision loupe, including on saved scans.
- Use document filters, brightness and contrast controls, markup, and page organization to prepare a scan.
- Recognize text from scanned pages with on-device OCR for supported languages.

## Read, organize, and protect scans

- Open saved PDFs and images in the built-in viewer, with zoom and page navigation.
- Keep scans on the device and protect selected files with the optional encrypted vault.
- Optionally back up scans to Google Drive.

These features make Narzary Scan useful for digitizing books, forms, identity documents, and other paper records.
