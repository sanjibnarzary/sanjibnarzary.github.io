## Full Description
FieldCam GPS is the ultimate field photography camera app for engineers, surveyors, construction managers, environmental scientists, real estate professionals, and outdoor enthusiasts.

Imprint accurate geographic coordinates, real-time altitude, magnetic compass heading, street address, timestamp, and customizable watermarks directly onto your photos and videos as you capture them.

---

### 🌟 TOP FEATURES:
• Real-Time GPS Location Stamp: High-precision latitude & longitude down to 6 decimal places.
• Telemetry HUD: Real-time altitude (meters/feet), speed (km/h or mph), and compass bearing.
• MiniMap Card: Embedded vector map stamp with customizable transparency.
• Custom Watermarks & Copyright: Protect your images with company copyright notices or client identifiers.
• Landscape & Portrait Optimized: Smart layout adapts to device orientation with dedicated left-side telemetry and right-side notes in landscape.
• Agency Emblems & Field Notes: Embed department crests and custom project notes directly onto images.
• 100% Privacy-Focused: GPS coordinates and photos are processed strictly on your device. No third-party tracking.
