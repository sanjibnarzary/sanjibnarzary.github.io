# Privacy Policy

This page summarizes the data handling that can be determined from the available CineCam AR Pro product information.

## Camera, tracking, and captured media

Camera access is required for the live viewfinder and capture. The app describes pose tracking with Google ML Kit and compositing selected AR layers and watermarks into photos and videos. Captures are registered with Android's media gallery.

## Microphone and other permissions

The product information describes optional audio feedback and video recording. Microphone access is needed if audio is recorded with a video; use the app's audio controls and Android permission settings to manage access.

The available product brief does not specify whether diagnostics, analytics, or other data are transmitted or how long application data is retained. Review the installed app's permission prompts and current privacy disclosures.
