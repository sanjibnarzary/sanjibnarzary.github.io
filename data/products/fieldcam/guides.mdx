# User Guides

Step-by-step guides for getting the most out of FieldCam: GPS Camera.

## Taking a stamped photo

Open the camera, wait for the GPS lock, then tap the shutter. The coordinates, address, date and time are stamped on the image automatically.

## Customizing the stamp

Open Settings to choose which details appear on the stamp, add a custom watermark and pick the MiniMap style.
