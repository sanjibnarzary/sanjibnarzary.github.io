## Camera controls

- Use focus peaking to highlight sharp edges in the live preview, adjusting its sensitivity and color.
- Set ISO, shutter speed, exposure compensation, white balance, and manual focus.
- Adjust zoom and exposure with the on-viewfinder gesture control.

## Pose tracking and AR

CineCam uses Google ML Kit pose detection to track body landmarks and align virtual wardrobe items and other AR accessories to a person in the camera view. A wireframe skeleton overlay can be enabled while tracking.

## Capture and save

Capture photos and videos with active AR layers and watermarks composited into the media. The app supports JPEG and RAW photos and MP4 video, and registers saved captures with the system media gallery.
