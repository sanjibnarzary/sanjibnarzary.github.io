# About CineCam AR Pro

CineCam AR Pro is an Android camera and augmented reality studio. Its camera tools include focus peaking and manual exposure controls, while pose tracking supports aligning virtual wardrobe items and other effects with a person in view.

## Camera and AR tools

- Tune ISO, shutter speed, exposure compensation, white balance, focus, and zoom.
- Use pose tracking and an optional wireframe skeleton overlay.
- Capture photos and videos with selected AR layers and watermarks composited into the output.
- Find captured media in the system gallery.
