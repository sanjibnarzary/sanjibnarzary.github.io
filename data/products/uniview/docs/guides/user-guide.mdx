> **Universal Document, Office, Code, and Image Suite for Android**

This handbook provides practical guides and workflows for utilizing the full feature set of Narzary Doc Viewer.

## 📑 1. Word & Document Viewer & In-Built Editor (.docx, .doc, .rtf)

### Viewing & Page-by-Page Reading
- Documents are rendered page-by-page as realistic white sheets with running headers, page numbers, and soft shadows.
- Formatted elements appear in high fidelity:
  - Titles, Main Sections (H1), and Subsections (H2).
  - Paragraphs with custom alignments (Left, Center, Right, and Justified).
  - Bulleted lists (`•`) and auto-numbered sequences (`1.`, `2.`).
  - Embedded data tables (3×3 grid with custom cell values).
  - Analytical graphs (bar charts with numeric metrics).
  - System architecture diagrams with flowchart nodes.

### In-Built Live Editing
- **Direct On-Page Editing:** Tap directly into any paragraph, heading, or table cell on any page to begin typing immediately.
- **Top Formatting Toolbar:**
  - **Styles Dropdown:** Switch current block between Title, Section (H1), Subsection (H2), and Normal Body.
  - **Text Styling:** Toggle **B** (Bold), *I* (Italic), <u>U</u> (Underline), and ~~S~~ (Strikethrough).
  - **Text Alignments:** Align Left, Center, Right, or Justify.
  - **Lists:** Quickly insert Bulleted Item or Numbered Item.
  - **Color Palette:** Select from Google Docs style colors (Slate, Google Blue, Crimson, Emerald Green, Purple, Amber).
  - **Font Size:** Increment or decrement font size in sp.
- **Inserting Elements:** Tap the `+` icon on the toolbar to append new Headings, Paragraphs, Bullet points, 3×3 Data Tables, Analytical Graphs, Architecture Diagrams, or Add a New Blank Page.
- **Saving:** Tap the Save icon (💾) in the top bar to save your edits locally.

## 📊 2. Excel & Spreadsheets (.xlsx, .xls, .csv)

### Smooth 2D Scrolling & Selection Matrix
- **Unblocked 2D Scrolling:** Scroll horizontally through endless columns (A to Z+) and vertically through thousands of rows with zero gesture conflicts.
- **Sticky Headers:** Row indices (#) remain pinned on the left, and column letters (A, B, C...) remain pinned at the top while scrolling.
- **Multi-Sheet Tabs:** Switch seamlessly between workbook sheets using the sheet tab bar. Tap `+` to add new sheets.

### Google Sheets Style Cell Editing & Formula Bar
- **Formula Bar:** Tapping any cell displays its exact formula or value in the formula bar above the grid.
- **Formula Shortcuts:** Quick chips for `=SUM()`, `=AVERAGE()`, `=COUNT()`, `=MAX()`, `=MIN()`, and `=IF()`.
- **Direct Cell Dialog:** Double-tap any cell to open the direct cell edit dialog with quick clear and save buttons.
- **Zooming:** Use the dedicated Zoom In (+) and Zoom Out (-) buttons in the top bar to adjust table font scale.

## 📽️ 3. Presentations & Slides (.pptx, .ppt)

### Dual-Mode Presentation Experience
1. **Vertical Slides Feed:** Continuous feed of presentation slides with titles, bullet items, slide numbers, and presenter notes.
2. **Theater Presentation Mode:**
   - Tap the **Present** icon (Slideshow 📽️) in the top bar.
   - Launches a fullscreen cinematic stage with a pure black background.
   - Shows slides **one by one** with smooth slide-in/slide-out horizontal animations.
   - **Slide Navigation:** Tap Previous (◀) and Next (▶) arrows or swipe left/right across the screen.
   - **Auto-Play Timer:** Tap Play (▶) to start automated 5-second slide transitions; tap Pause (⏸) to halt.
   - **Progress Indicator:** Live slide counter (`Slide X of Y`) and progress bar show your position in the presentation.
   - **Exiting:** Press the back button or tap the close (✕) button to exit presentation mode.

## 🖼️ 4. Image Viewer & Google Photos Style Editor

### Image Viewer Mode
- Fullscreen dark backdrop with pinch-to-zoom (up to 400%) and double-tap zoom toggle.
- **Star & Favorites:** Tap the Star icon (★) in the top bar or bottom bar to mark favorite photos.
- **Share:** Tap Share to send photos directly to other apps.
- **Info Dialog:** Inspect resolution, dimensions, file size, and sandboxed storage path.

### Google Photos Style Editing Suite
Tap the **Edit** button in the bottom bar to open the editing workspace:
1. **Crop Tab:**
   - Aspect ratio presets: Free, Square (1:1), 16:9, 4:3, 3:2.
   - Custom crop boundaries with real-time preview and Reset.
2. **Rotate & Flip Tab:**
   - Quick 90° clockwise rotation.
   - Horizontal and Vertical flip toggles.
   - Fine rotation angle slider (-45° to +45°).
3. **Resize Tab:**
   - Preset scaling chips: 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100% (Original).
   - Custom width & height pixel inputs with automatic aspect ratio maintenance.
4. **Adjust Tab:**
   - **Brightness:** -50 to +50 slider.
   - **Contrast:** 0.5× to 2.0× slider.
   - **Saturation:** 0.0× (Monochrome Grayscale) to 2.0× (Vivid Color) slider.
5. **Multi-Format Save:**
   - Tap **Save** to choose export format: **PNG (Lossless)**, **JPEG / JPG**, or **WEBP (Modern)**.
   - Select compression quality (10% to 100%).
   - Choose whether to save as a new copy or overwrite the original file.
   - *Free tier saves with a subtle watermark; Pro users receive pristine watermark-free exports.*

## 🔒 5. Password-Protected PDFs & Continuous Viewer

- **Password Prompt:** When opening an encrypted PDF, Narzary Doc Viewer automatically detects encryption and prompts with a secure password dialog.
- **Password Visibility:** Toggle the eye icon to verify typed characters.
- **Continuous Multi-Page Scroll:** Unlocked PDFs render in high-resolution multi-page continuous vertical feed.
- **Hand Gestures:** Smooth pinch-to-zoom and double-tap to reset/zoom.

## 💻 6. Programming Code & Text Viewer

- **Supported Languages:** C, C++, Java, Kotlin, Python, JSON, XML, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, Bash, and Plain Text.
- **Syntax Highlighting:** Real-time semantic coloring for keywords, types, strings, comments, numbers, and annotations.
- **Gutter Line Numbers:** Sticky line number column synchronized with vertical scrolling.
- **Hand Gesture Font Zoom:** Pinch with two fingers to increase or decrease code font size smoothly.
- **JSON Pretty-Printer:** Open any minified `.json` file and tap the AutoFix (✨) icon in the top bar to format with 2-space indentation.
- **Search:** Tap the Search icon (🔍) to highlight occurrences of any variable or token.

## 🪟 7. Multi-Window Intent Architecture & Clean Back Navigation

- **System Open With:** Tap any document in Files, WhatsApp, Gmail, or Browser and choose **Narzary Doc Viewer**.
- **Separate Intent Windows:** Word, Excel, PPT, PDF, Code, and Images open in their own dedicated activity task windows in the Android Recent Apps carousel.
- **Clean Back Navigation:** Clicking the top-left back arrow (←) or pressing the Android hardware back button / swipe back gesture **immediately closes the document viewer intent** and returns directly to the calling application.
