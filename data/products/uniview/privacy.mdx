# Privacy Policy

Uniview is described as a local-first document viewer and manager. Documents, images, and source files may contain personal or confidential information.

## Files you open

Files can be selected from the app or opened through Android's “Open with” flow. The product brief describes local file viewing and editing. It does not specify whether every file operation stays on-device or identify any external services, so review the app's current disclosures before opening sensitive files.

## Permissions and sharing

Android may ask you to grant access to selected files or other features. Exporting or sharing a file sends it to the destination you select. Manage app permissions and stored files through Android settings and the relevant file tools.

The available product information does not specify analytics, diagnostics, or data retention beyond its local-first description.
