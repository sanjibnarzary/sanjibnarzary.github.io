# About Kidz Learn & Draw

Kidz Learn & Draw combines picture tracing, geometric-shape drawing, coloring, and learning games for children. The product description also highlights offline parent progress tracking.

## Creative learning activities

Children can explore drawing and coloring through guided activities, while parents can review progress offline.

## Developer
