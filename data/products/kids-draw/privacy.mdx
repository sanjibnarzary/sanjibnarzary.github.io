# Privacy Policy

The available Kidz Learn & Draw product information describes tracing, drawing, coloring, learning games, and offline parent progress tracking.

## Progress and device access

Parent progress tracking is described as offline. The product brief does not state what information is included in progress records, where they are stored, or how long they are retained. Review the app's permission requests and in-app privacy disclosures before use, especially when the app is used by children.

## Third-party services

The available product information does not identify analytics, advertising, or third-party data services. This page does not make a claim that none are used; consult the installed app's current disclosures for complete details.

Parents or guardians should supervise a child's use and manage app permissions through Android settings.
