## Draw and learn

Kidz Learn & Draw combines picture tracing, geometric-shape drawing, coloring, and learning games in one app. Children can use these activities to explore drawing and practice creative skills.

## Parent progress

The supplied product information describes offline parent progress tracking, so progress can be reviewed without requiring an online account.
