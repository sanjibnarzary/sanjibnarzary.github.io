# Privacy Policy

YogaPose is described as a guide to yoga poses with instructions and visual aids. The available product information does not describe collection, storage, permissions, analytics, or third-party services.

Review Android permission prompts and the app's current in-app privacy disclosures before use. If you share information from the app, it is sent to the destination you choose.

If the app offers an in-app purchase, the payment provider displayed at checkout processes the transaction under its own privacy policy.
