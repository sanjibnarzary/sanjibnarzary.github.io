## 1. Description of Service

## 3. Cryptographic Non-Repudiation & DSC Verification

1. Signatures generated using your local private key establish mathematical proof of authenticity for the associated SHA-256 document digest.
2. TrueSeal's Universal DSC Verifier inspects and validates digital signatures embedded in PDFs according to standard RFC 3161 and PKCS#7 / PAdES formats. TrueSeal reports signature integrity as computed mathematically from byte-range coverage and certificate metadata; it does not replace statutory notary or judicial determination where physical presence or state-licensed oaths are required by local law.
