# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Narzary GPS Camera Map handles information used for location-stamped captures.

## Camera and location

Camera access is used to capture photos and videos. Location is used to add coordinates, address, and map context to a capture. The product information describes location and media processing on the device; location should be used only while capturing or performing an action that needs it.

## Saved media and permissions

Captures and their embedded location details are saved as media on your device. You can manage or delete those files using the app and your device's gallery or file tools. Camera, location, and microphone permissions can be changed in Android settings; microphone access is relevant when recording video audio.

## Payments and external services

The app's product information describes no third-party advertising or analytics/tracking and says location and media are processed on the device. If you make a purchase, Google Play processes the transaction; payment credentials are handled by Google Play. Check the app's current disclosures for the installed version.
