# About Narzary GPS Camera Map

Narzary GPS Camera Map adds location context to photos and videos as they are captured. Stamps can include GPS coordinates, altitude, compass heading, address, date and time, a MiniMap, and custom watermarks.

## Made for field documentation

The app is intended for field notes, inspections, travel records, and other situations where the place and time of capture are useful context. Location and media are processed on the device, as described in the product information.
