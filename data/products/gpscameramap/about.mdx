# About Narzary GPS Camera Map

Narzary GPS Camera Map is an Android camera app that stamps your photos and videos with GPS coordinates, altitude, compass heading, address, timestamp, a MiniMap and custom watermarks. It is built for people who need to prove where and when an image was captured: surveyors, field engineers, inspectors, environmental researchers and outdoor professionals.

## Our principles

- **Ad-free, always:** no ads, banners, pop-ups or third-party trackers.
- **Private by design:** GPS data and media are processed and stored on your device.
- **Independent:** built and maintained by a single developer and supported by Pro subscriptions and voluntary donations.

## Built with

- Jetpack Compose (100% Kotlin) and Material Design 3
- CameraX for capture
- Google Play Services Location
- Google Play Billing for subscriptions
