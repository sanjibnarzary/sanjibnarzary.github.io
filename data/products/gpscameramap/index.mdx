## Location on every capture

The camera adds GPS coordinates, altitude, compass heading, a reverse-geocoded address, and a timestamp to captured photos and videos. A MiniMap provides a visual reference for where the image was taken.

## For field documentation

Narzary GPS Camera Map is suited to field notes, inspections, travel records, and other situations where an image needs location context. Custom watermarks can add an identifying mark to the captured media.

Location and media processing are performed on the device, according to the product information provided for the app.
