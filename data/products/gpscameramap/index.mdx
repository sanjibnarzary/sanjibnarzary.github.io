## Top features

- **Real-time GPS stamp:** latitude and longitude down to 6 decimal places, with reverse-geocoded address and timestamp.
- **Telemetry HUD:** altitude (meters or feet), speed (km/h or mph) and magnetic or true compass bearing.
- **MiniMap card:** an embedded vector map stamp with adjustable transparency.
- **Custom watermarks and copyright:** protect images with a copyright notice or client identifier, placed in any of the four corners with adjustable opacity.
- **Landscape and portrait layouts:** in landscape, the MiniMap and telemetry sit on the left and your survey note is pinned to the right.
- **Agency emblems and field notes:** add department crests and project notes to every image.
- **Privacy first:** GPS coordinates and photos are processed on your device. There are no ads and no third-party trackers.
